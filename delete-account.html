Delete your WorkoutSocial account

You can permanently delete your WorkoutSocial account and its data at any time.

Fastest way — in the app

  1. Open WorkoutSocial and log in
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Can't access the app?

Email socialworkout73@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll verify it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account: profile (username, email), workout logs and proof photos/videos, pod membership, chat posts and reactions, streak history, and notification tokens. Nothing is retained after deletion, and it cannot be undone.
